All three customer-supplied banners are 3840×800, clean, text-free, and approved for full-bleed use. They must remain image backgrounds with real DOM copy and controls above them.

| Banner | Subject / focal zone | Desktop copy and object position | 390px strategy |

| `轮播图/3.jpg` | Needle/cannula macro detail sweeps from left toward center; luminous negative space at right | Copy on right; `object-position: 44% 50%`; localized right-side darkening only where needed | Preserve the needle tip and leading lines; `object-position: 43% 50%`; if crop loses the tip, use image-above/copy-below composition |

Global requirements: no white card over the hero, no independent framed image, no generated text inside images, keyboard/touch controls, pause control, and reduced-motion static first slide.
